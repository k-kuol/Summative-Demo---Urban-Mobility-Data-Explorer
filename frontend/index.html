The slower the city, the more each mile costs

Taxis average about 23 mph at 5 am but crawl near 11 mph from 8 am to 6 pm. Because the meter also charges for time stuck in traffic, the price of a mile climbs as speed falls.

Thursday and Friday evenings are the city’s peak

Rides on an average day, by weekday and hour. Weekday mornings are busy but slow; the true peak comes between 6 and 8 pm late in the week.

Rides per day across January
